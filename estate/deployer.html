Every number the game is tuned by, with where it lives and whether a change reached the game. This page sets no values of its own.

Where it lives: ON CHAIN the contract is the truth IN A SEED frozen into a map, reproducible from it CLIENT BELIEF the game believes it; nothing enforces it OUR SERVER the game as it is played, held on our server; the chain keeps its record, not its state NO HOME not in schema.json at all
What the value is: DECIDED settled in DESIGN.md, and cited READ FROM THE CONTRACT read off the chain, which is the only place that can verify it READ FROM THE GAME not typed here — read out of what runs, which is not the chain PROPOSED a proposal on another page, not a decision NOT DECIDED empty, and it stays empty
A number on this page is one of three things and never a fourth: a decision cited from DESIGN.md, a value read live out of the game and never typed into this file, or blank because nobody has decided it. The homes are read out of schema.json, which is the one place a piece of state declares where it lives.
