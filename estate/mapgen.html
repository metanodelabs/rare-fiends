HOW THE GENERATOR WORKS
  1. Size from the players: each needs a base (36 tiles) plus frontier, about 170 land tiles each.
  2. The island: fractal noise with a warped edge for bays and headlands. The sea level leaves about half the square as land; stray islets sink, and inland water becomes lakes.
  3. Height: land is ranked into shore, lowland, plain, hill, high ground and peak, with ridged noise making the hills.
  4. Rivers run from the high ground down to the sea and carve valleys either side.
  5. Bases: one plot per player, the standard base shape, spread out, on buildable ground, clear of the core.
  6. Creeks: every base gets running water. A creek rises just uphill and runs down through it (the generator needs it).
  7. Forests grow where a second noise field (moisture) is high, on low ground and hills.
  8. Ruins are scarce (about one per 14 players), far from bases, each with glitching terminals for later levels.
  9. Turning: drag across to turn it freely, like the base (the MOVE / TURN switch picks what a plain drag does; right-drag or Shift does the other); ⟲ ⟳ turn 45°, N points north up, a two-finger twist turns it on a phone.
  10. Stepped access: a rise of two or more levels is a cliff Friends can't climb. Every raised area must have a way up in steps of one; any that doesn't gets a staircase cut into its side.

The algorithm is estate/mapgen.js: the same file a new game will call with its seed. Trees and terminals are the FriendSDK's props (Apache-2.0).
